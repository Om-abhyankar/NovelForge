Write your novel, right here, free.

No account. Nothing uploaded. Your writing is saved to this browser as you type, and you can export it as real Word documents any time — take that file home, open it again later, or bring it into the full desktop app.

This is a lightweight companion to NovelForge, a free desktop app for Windows with maps, outline frameworks, a continuity checker and more. This browser version covers the core writing loop only — chapters, scenes, and export to real .docx files.
